### Goal
To prepare lab materials and process metabolomic extractions on multiple life stages of *M. capitata* samples for the Symbiont Integration experiment using the [larval protocol](/notebook/2020-02-03-metabolomics-p-astreoides-sample-prep.md).

### Laboratory preparation

1. Autoclaved all glass ware.
2. Wiped down all counter tops and fumehood with 10% bleach solution.
3. Obtained dry ice.
4. Took aliquots of master extraction buffer and 15% ammonium bicarbonate solutions for today's extractions.
